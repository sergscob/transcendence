Privacy Policy

Project: Transcendence (42 school project) | Last updated: April 7, 2026

This project is an educational student project developed in the context of 42 Paris. It is not a commercial service. This document is provided for transparency and project compliance purposes.

1. Who We Are

Transcendence is a web application created by students as part of the 42 curriculum. If you use this instance, your data is processed only for educational and technical operation of the platform.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: username, email, password hash.
  • Authentication data: login tokens and optional 2FA related data.
  • OAuth data: identifiers returned by Google or 42 OAuth providers.
  • Profile data: avatar, biography/description, and other profile fields you provide.
  • Social/game data: friends, invitations, gameplay state, and related metadata.
  • Chat data: messages exchanged in chat features.
  • Technical data: logs required for debugging, security, and maintenance.

3. How We Use Data

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To authenticate users and secure access.
  • To provide game, chat, profile, and social features.
  • To detect abuse and maintain platform integrity.
  • To debug and improve the project in an educational context.

4. Local Storage and Cookies

The frontend may store technical values such as auth token, language preference, and server settings in browser storage (for example localStorage) to keep your session and preferences.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by third-party providers only when needed for authentication or infrastructure used by this project (for example Google OAuth or 42 OAuth).

6. Data Retention

Data is kept as long as needed for the project operation, evaluation, and maintenance, or until deletion is requested and technically feasible in this educational setup.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures for an educational project. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Minors

This project is intended for educational use by students and evaluators. If you are a minor, use should be under school and legal guardian guidance where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated during the project lifecycle. The latest version is the one published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy requests, contact the project maintainers/team operating this specific instance.
